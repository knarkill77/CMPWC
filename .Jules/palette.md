This journal tracks critical UX and accessibility learnings discovered during the development of the Elite 8 Duals website.

**Learning:** Initializing the journal to track project-specific UX patterns and accessibility improvements.
**Action:** Consistently document any unique challenges or highly effective UX solutions encountered in this repo.

## 2025-05-14 - Navigation Accessibility & UX Polish
**Learning:** Adding a "Skip to content" link as the first focusable element significantly improves the experience for keyboard users. Smooth scrolling provides a much more natural transition between sections compared to abrupt jumps. Interactive mailto links with clear hover states improve the discoverability of contact methods.
**Action:** Always include a skip link on landing pages and ensure interactive elements have visual feedback that matches the brand's design system.
