## 2026-06-22 - [Accessibility & Micro-UX Enhancements]
**Learning:** Implemented "Skip to content" link, mobile menu ARIA attributes, and decorative icon hiding. The "Skip to content" pattern is essential for dark themes and requires high-contrast focus styles.
**Action:** Always include ARIA labels and expanded states for toggle components like mobile menus. Use `aria-hidden="true"` for decorative icons to reduce screen reader noise.
