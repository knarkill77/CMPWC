## 2026-06-11 - [Skip to content accessibility pattern]
**Learning:** Adding a "Skip to content" link is a critical accessibility requirement for keyboard users in long landing pages. Using a standard `.sr-only` utility that becomes visible on focus ensures it doesn't impact the visual design while remaining discoverable.
**Action:** Always include a skip link targeting `id="main-content"` on the primary content container in future landing page enhancements.
